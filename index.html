About us

A light for the community, lit through knowledge and service.

Light of Guidance Foundation is a non-profit organisation dedicated to promoting religion, education, charity, social welfare and economic upliftment, particularly among members of the Shia Ithna Ashari community.

We organise religious and educational programmes in person and online, produce and share videos, publications and digital content, and stand beside the deserving through scholarships, welfare and livelihood support.

Let’s Illuminate Our Imaan – Through the Qur’an & Ahlulbait (a.s.)
  • FaithProgrammes, publications and institutions for advanced religious education.
  • LearningScholarships, educational assistance, counselling and career guidance.
  • ServiceMedical, housing and essential aid, and support for widows, orphans and families in need.

Every donation and grant we receive is used exclusively for these non-profit objects.
